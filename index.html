How It's Made

1

Design

I find or make a 3D model on the computer.

2

Slice

Bambu Studio cuts the model into hundreds of thin layers.

3

Print

The printer melts plastic and builds it one layer at a time.

4

Check

I clean it up and test it before it goes to you.

💡 Fun fact: I print with PLA, a plastic made from plants like corn!

How to Order

  1. Ask a grown-up first. A parent needs to say it's OK.
  2. Fill out the order form with the item, color, and your first name only.
  3. We'll email your parent to confirm the price and when it'll be ready.
  4. Pick it up and pay in person. No credit cards on this site!

Questions

How long does an order take?

"In stock" items are usually ready in a few days. "Made to order" items take about a week.

How do I pay?

Payment happens in person between grown-ups. This website never asks for card numbers.

Are the prints strong?

Yes for normal play! But keep them out of hot cars and away from the dishwasher, because PLA can bend when it gets too hot.

Are they safe for little kids?

Some items are small and could be a choking hazard. They are not for kids under 3.

Can I get a color that isn't listed?

Maybe! Ask on the order form and I'll check what filament I have.
